Past Simple
and Present Perfect

The perfect reports a present situation. The simple locates a finished time. Practice uses adult contexts: bids, accounts, visits, news. The slides tell the story; this page is the work.

Present perfect

Result now, just / already / yet, experience, an open week or month.

Past simple

in March, at 5.30, ago, a When-question, a period the speaker has closed.

REFERENCE

Three blocks, not a marker list

What the perfect does, what the simple does, then only the collision.

  1. A present situation. The courier has lost the samples. We do not have them. The time is not the point.
  2. just, already, yet. I've just come off a call. We've already filed the bid. Have you finalized the slot yet?
  3. Experience up to now. Have you ever pitched for a public contract? It's the first time we've dealt with this supplier.
  4. An open period. so far, this month, today while it is still today. We've had four client calls so far this week.
  1. A finished time. We signed the framework agreement in March. The results were announced last week.
  2. A story. She called head office at 5.30, explained the delay and sent the revised brief.
  3. When / What time / How long ago. These expect a finished answer. When did you speak to them? — On Tuesday.
  4. A posting that ended. I worked on the Seoul account for three years, then moved to audit in 2022.
  1. News, then detail. Have you heard about the bid? We heard this morning. They announced it at 9.
  2. The clock moves. Wednesday: Have you spoken to the caterers this week? Friday, 5 p.m.: Did you speak to the caterers this week?
  3. for / since against ago. She has managed the account for six years. Not with two weeks ago.
  4. gone / been / went. still there, back, or a dated trip.
  5. British default with just / already / yet is the perfect. American English often uses the past simple. Viewpoint can close this week before midnight.

PRACTICE

Use the context, not the highlighter

The time marker is sometimes a trap. Read who is speaking, and whether the period is still open.
